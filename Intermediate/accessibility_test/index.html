How to test on Windows

  • Narrator (built in): press Ctrl+Win+Enter to turn it on or off. Press H to jump between headings and Tab to move between links. In scan mode, use the arrow keys to read each chart.
  • NVDA (free, nvaccess.org): press H for headings and G to jump to the next graphic.
  • Edge and Chrome work best with both. Listen first, then open "Expected text" on each page to compare.
